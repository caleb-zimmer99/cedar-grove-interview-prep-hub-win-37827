## About Cedar Grove Prep

Cedar Grove Prep is a lightweight interview preparation web app built around a simple HTML-first structure. It is meant for people who want a straightforward place to review interview material, practice answers, and keep notes together without depending on a heavy runtime.

Because it is browser-based and friendly to static hosting, the project fits well into quick local setups and simple web deployments. That makes it a practical option for candidates, mentors, or teams who want a focused prep workspace that opens directly in the browser.
